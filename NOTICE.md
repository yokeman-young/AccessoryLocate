## Third-party references

The implementation studies public protocol behavior documented by:

- [youcci/playport](https://github.com/youcci/playport)
- [shihabal3amri/DiPlay](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay)
- [pymobiledevice3](https://github.com/doronz88/pymobiledevice3)

The optional importer can read a shared experimental identity from a user-
supplied DiPlay v0.2.7 APK. The APK and identity files are not distributed by
this repository. Their provenance and any redistribution restrictions remain
separate from this project's source-code license.

PlayPort and DiPlay are separate projects under their own licenses and notices.
This repository does not claim ownership of their code or identity material.
